## 🏢 Leadership

I'm the founder and CEO of **GreenSoftware**. Since 2009, the company has built and supported automation systems for government organizations.

- **Company:** I set product direction, own client relationships and run daily operations
- **Clients:** 50 government clients on long-term support contracts
- **Delivery:** from requirements and client feedback through release, support and maintenance
- **Systems over heroics:** I build processes and tooling so the business doesn't depend on any single person

## 🧑‍💻 Engineering

- **Trading systems:** MetaTrader Expert Advisors and LLM-driven trading research
- **AI desktop tools:** Windows apps that bring AI to whatever is on screen
